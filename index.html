The Resort

A brand-new five-star escape on the Red Sea

Amarina Jannah Resort & Aqua Park opened in 2023 in Coraya Bay, midway between Port Ghalib and El Quseir and just minutes from Marsa Alam International Airport.

Set directly on a sandy 175-metre private beach beside the celebrated Coraya Bay house reef, the resort blends all-inclusive comfort with one of the region's best aqua parks, a PADI dive centre, and a full wellness spa.

With 280 rooms, four restaurants and a lively daily entertainment programme, Amarina Jannah is built for families, couples and divers alike.
