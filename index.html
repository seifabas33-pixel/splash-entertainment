Sahl Hasheesh
About The Destination
Red Sea Riviera, Egypt

Sahl Hasheesh is one of Egypt's most exclusive coastal resorts, 18 km south of Hurghada. Its sheltered bay offers world-class snorkelling and diving in the Red Sea's warm, crystal-clear waters. The area is known for its pristine white sand beaches, vibrant coral reefs and year-round sunshine.
