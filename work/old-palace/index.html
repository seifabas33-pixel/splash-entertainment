Old Palace Resort

Guest entertainment platform

A guest platform for Old Palace Resort with the daily programme, concierge, excursions and feedback. It works offline.

The brief

Guests needed one place to see what’s on today, book excursions, ask for room services and tell management how their stay is going, in their own language and without installing anything.

What I built

An installable web app that keeps working without Wi-Fi. It has a 7-day programme with a live “Happening now” card, a personal “My Day” with reminders, a WhatsApp concierge, live weather, and staff tools to update the programme.

Key features

01

Happening now

Live countdowns to the next activity and tonight’s show, updated every minute.

02

My Day

Guests star activities and get a reminder 15 minutes before each one starts.

03

WhatsApp concierge

14 one-tap requests with the room number already filled in.

04

Works offline

Installs to the home screen and keeps working without Wi-Fi.

05

Four languages

English, Arabic, German and Russian, with a right-to-left layout for Arabic.

06

Staff tools

A staff sign-in with a printable programme, plus an editor for the programme, Wi-Fi details and excursions.
