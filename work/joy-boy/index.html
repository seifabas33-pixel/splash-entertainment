The brief

Resort managers decide fast. Joy Boy needed a site that shows the team on stage, proves results with awards and guest reviews, and makes it easy to start a conversation.

What I built

A bold, playful portfolio in English, German and Italian, with a spotlight photo wall, show reels, awards, guest quotes and an FAQ. Behind it is a private staff portal for shift check-in, rosters and guest feedback.

Key features

01

Spotlight hero

A drifting wall of show photos lit by a spotlight that follows your finger or cursor.

02

Show reels

Five reels from real programme nights: fire, Echo of Egypt, kids, light show and theme nights.

03

Awards & reviews

Booking.com and HolidayCheck awards next to real guest quotes.

04

Three languages

English, German and Italian pages, each with its own address for Google.

05

Staff portal

Google sign-in, location-checked shift check-in, rosters and payroll for the team.

06

Guest feedback by QR

QR cards lead guests to a feedback page that syncs to Google Sheets.
