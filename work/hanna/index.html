The brief

Hanna helps international buyers, many of them from Poland, buy property on the Red Sea. Buyers needed to trust her before the first call.

What I built

A light, glassy site in English and Polish with twelve verified listings, her four-step verification process, client testimonials and a contact form that writes the WhatsApp message for the buyer. It comes with a matching digital business card.

Key features

01

English & Polish

A full translation, including the animated headline.

02

Verified listings

12 properties across 4 developments, each with size, bedrooms and view.

03

Verification process

The four checks every property passes before Hanna shows it to a buyer.

04

WhatsApp or email

One form, two buttons. Each one writes the message for the buyer.

05

Digital business card

A separate card page she can share in one tap.

06

Motion

Particles, a typewriter headline, 3D tilt cards and count-up numbers.
