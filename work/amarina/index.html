The brief

Guests arrive from all over Europe and need one place on their phone for restaurants, opening times, activities and tonight’s show, in their own language and with no app to download.

What I built

A fast web app that installs from the browser. Every restaurant and bar shows whether it is open right now, the evening programme has a card for each night, and the gallery uses the resort’s real photography. WhatsApp is one tap away on every screen.

Key features

01

Eight languages

English, Arabic, Dutch, German, Polish, Czech, French and Russian, with a right-to-left layout for Arabic.

02

Live opening status

Each restaurant and bar shows Open or Closed from the current time, refreshed every minute.

03

Evening programme

One card for each night of the week, so guests can plan their stay around the shows.

04

Dining guide

Restaurants and bars in two tabs, with cuisine, location and meal times.

05

Add to home screen

Installs like an app straight from the browser. No app store needed.

06

WhatsApp contact

A floating button and a contact card for questions and requests.
