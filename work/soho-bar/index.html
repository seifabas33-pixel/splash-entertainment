The brief

Soho Bar is rated number one in Port Ghalib, but tourists in nearby hotels had no easy way to see the menu, learn about the free hotel pick-up or book a table.

What I built

A warm dark-and-gold site in three languages: the menu in five tabs, ratings from TripAdvisor and Google on the first screen, a map, and a booking form that turns into a ready-written WhatsApp message.

Key features

01

Booking by WhatsApp

Name, date, time, guests and hotel for the free pick-up, sent as one ready-made WhatsApp message.

02

Menu in five tabs

Grill, tapas, Italian pizzas, vegan, and bar & cocktails.

03

Three languages

English, German and Italian, chosen automatically from the visitor’s phone.

04

Ratings up front

TripAdvisor, Google and Restaurant Guru scores on the first screen.

05

Find us

A built-in map and the opening hours, every day 10:30 to midnight.

06

Ready for Google

Restaurant details are marked up so search results can show hours and prices.
