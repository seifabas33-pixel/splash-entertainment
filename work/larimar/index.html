The brief

Mohamed sells villas and suites at Larimar to buyers abroad. He needed one link he can send to every lead that explains the projects and makes it easy to ask for prices.

What I built

A clean navy-and-teal portfolio with three project cards, life at Port Ghalib, five reasons to invest, and a contact form that sends a WhatsApp message with the chosen project.

Key features

01

Project cards

Villas, Suites and Larimar 3, each with unit sizes and a “Request pricing” button.

02

WhatsApp enquiries

Pick a project and the form writes the WhatsApp message.

03

Why invest

Five clear reasons, from turnkey delivery and rental demand to hotel management and payment plans.

04

Lifestyle

Six cards about life in Port Ghalib.

05

Motion

A particle loader, parallax hero, 3D tilt cards and count-up numbers.

06

Share-ready

A custom preview image for WhatsApp and social media, and it saves to the iPhone home screen.
