About Me

Fresh Energy, Genuine Commitment

Hi, I'm Hanna Różczka — a real estate consultant at Homes Bay Hurghada. I joined the team with one clear mission: to give international buyers the honest, transparent guidance they deserve when exploring Egypt's stunning Red Sea property market.

Whether you're looking for a holiday retreat, a rental investment, or a permanent move, I take the time to truly understand what matters to you — and match you only with properties that have passed Homes Bay's rigorous 4-step verification process. No pressure, no shortcuts.

As a Polish speaker, I'm especially well-placed to support Polish buyers navigating every step — from first enquiry to key handover.
